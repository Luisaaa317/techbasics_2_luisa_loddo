# learnings

• a buzzer can be active and passive
	◦ a passive buzzer needs signal to make sound
	◦ an active buzzer can make sound just by being connected to a power source
	◦ longer leg of active buzzer is the plus
• there are example codes for libraries which can be found under file/example/(name of the library)
• an ultrasonic distance sensor has a transmitter that gives a signal, the signal is repelled by another object and received by the receiver part of the sensor
	◦ the smallest distance it can measure is 3cm
• a button has lines on the bottom that signal how the legs are connected, can be used to connect the breadboard across the spine
