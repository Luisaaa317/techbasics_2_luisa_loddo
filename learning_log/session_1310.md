From the first session of the semester I recall that there are a variety of opportunities to explore when working with Arduino. 
One can create projects that include blinking lights or even tactile sensory stimuli (such as a calming anxiety bracelet that massages you). 
In the first session I/we learned how to control the speed with which LEDs blink. 
One important thing I recall is that when making changes to the board one works with, the board should always be disconnected from the laptop. 
Another thing I recall is to never connect the plus nd the minus. Else your board could break which is something one should try to prevent seeing as the board is the most expensive part of the kit.
So far there hasn’t been anything I’ve struggled with.
