# Topics
-	Different types of circuits (series and parallel)
-	Color codes of resistors
-	Breadboard
-	RGB LED
-	New function analogWrite 

# Learnings
-	Parallel circuit is more stable meaning that even if one component breaks the other will still work, this is not the case with a serial cicuit
-	The long leg of the RGB LED is the anode or the cathode, in the case of our starter kit it is a cathode, the other three legs are responsible for the red, green and blue light
-	The RGB LED can be coded with the function analogWrite to light up in different colorshades 
-	The breadboard can be powered by either Arduino board or a power module such as a battery
